The official Notion CLI (`ntn`) is installed. Use the `notion-cli` skill for Notion work. Its wrapper supplies a non-secret authentication marker; the credential gateway injects the workspace token for authorized agents. Do not run `ntn login`, store tokens locally, or reinstall the CLI. Nano and Lab have operator-authorized read/write access to the connected workspace. Read access does not imply an instruction to change workspace content; follow the user's task.
